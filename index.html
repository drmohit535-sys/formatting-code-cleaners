Frequently Asked Questions

What is a code formatter?

A code formatter is a tool that automatically reorganizes and beautifies your code by fixing indentation, spacing, and structure. It makes your code more readable and professional-looking without changing its functionality.

Are these tools free?

Yes! All our formatting tools are completely free to use. No hidden charges, no subscriptions, and no premium features. We believe in providing quality tools accessible to everyone.

Is my code secure?

Absolutely! Your code is never stored on our servers. All processing happens directly in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your data remains completely private and secure at all times.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes! Our tools are fully responsive and work perfectly on mobile devices, tablets, and desktops. You can format code on the go without any issues.

Do I need an account?

No account needed! You can use all our tools immediately without any registration. Just visit the website and start formatting your code right away.

Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Formatting & Code Cleaners. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our services.

We prioritize your privacy. Here's what we collect:

  • Code formatting requests are processed entirely in your browser
  • No code or formatted content is transmitted to our servers
  • We do not collect personal information unless you voluntarily provide it through contact forms
  • Basic analytics data to improve our service (anonymized and aggregated)

Cookies Policy

Our website uses minimal cookies:
